Crafty Synth for macOS: AU, VST3 and a standalone app (Apple Silicon and Intel)
================================================================================

1. Copy the plug-ins:
     Crafty Synth.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
     Crafty Synth.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   (Crafty Synth.app can go anywhere, e.g. /Applications.)

2. They are not notarised (no paid Apple developer account), so macOS blocks them at first.
   In Terminal, once:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Crafty Synth.component"
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Crafty Synth.vst3"
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Crafty Synth.app"

3. Restart your DAW (Ableton Live: Preferences -> Plug-Ins -> Rescan).

Your user presets and wavetables are kept in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Twisted Genetics/Crafty Synth/Crafty Synth.user
shared by every instance, and in the FM-1's own format.

Manual: https://github.com/TwistedGenetics/Crafty-Synth/blob/main/docs/PORTS.md
