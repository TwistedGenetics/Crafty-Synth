Crafty Synth

A CRAFTsynth 2.0-style monophonic wavetable synth for the M-VAVE FM-1: 8 oscillators, 16 oscillator modifiers, spread into unison or chords, a morphing filter, 3 envelopes, 2 LFOs, an 8-slot mod matrix, distortion, delay and a sequenced arpeggiator.

EditorEdit every value, hear it here (the firmware's own engine), manage the 64 presets and turn Serum / Vital wavetables into user banks. Try the demoThe editor with a simulated FM-1. Firmware flasherInstall Crafty Synth on the FM-1 from Chrome or Edge.

Beta, not yet tried on a real FM-1. Read the recovery guide before you install anything. Source: github.com/TwistedGenetics/Crafty-Synth. Free software, GPL-3.0. Not affiliated with M-VAVE.
